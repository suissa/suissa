SUISSA — Outcome Engineer and AI Multi-agents Architect

Jean Carlo Nascimento, known as SUISSA, is an Outcome Engineer and AI Multi-agents Architect focused on FullAgenticStack, AllasCode, Outcome as a Software, conversational automation, passwordless identity, semantic architectures, agentic runtimes, self-healing systems and open-source software.

Core concepts

FullAgenticStack
An agentic architecture connecting human intent, classification, orchestration, agents, multimodal interaction, A2UI, projections, observability and self-healing execution.
AllasCode
Human-intent-bound architecture for conversational automation. The central application acts primarily as classifier and orchestrator while domain behavior is represented semantically.
Outcome as a Software (OaaS)
A model focused on delivering and accepting outcomes rather than merely delivering software features.
Agent-Actor-Action (A3)
An ontology organized around AtomicSkill, AtomicBehavior, Action, Actor and Runtime.
Semantic-as-Code
Semantic contracts, declarations and implementation meaning are first-class artifacts of software development.
Trajectory-Driven Development (T-DD)
A verification-oriented chain: DESTINY → INTENT → BEHAVIOR → EVIDENCE → CONTRACT → STATE → ACTOR → SKILL → TRAJECTORY → DSL → IMPLEMENTATION.
2flow
A notation for representing intent-driven flows, behaviors and agentic execution.
PreCog / 2nd Brain
A second-brain model based on Experience → Consolidation → Knowledge → Capability → Behavior → Experience, including dreaming and knowledge maintenance.
Proactive by Construction
Proactivity is encoded into architecture and behavior rather than added as an isolated feature.
Compliance-by-Construction
Compliance constraints and boundaries are enforced by architectural design and execution paths.
Human-in-the-Healing-Loop
Self-healing systems automatically diagnose and repair where confidence is sufficient and escalate uncertain cases to human authority.
Human-Intent-Bound Architecture
System authority and execution are bounded by the user's intent and authorization.
eXtreme Zero Trust
Continuous verification and constrained authority across identity, communication, agents and execution.
Passwordless Identity
Identity based on cryptographic proofs and trusted interaction rather than passwords.
Outcome Acceptance
A contract defining the evidence required to accept that an intended outcome has been achieved.
Solution as a Service
A service model centered on solving a problem and producing an accepted outcome.
User-First
The user's intent, authority and desired outcome are the primary system boundaries.
Devir
A SUISSA concept describing evolutionary development across behavior, agents and runtime systems.
Agentic Runtime
Runtime infrastructure for stateful agents, communication, supervision, execution, memory and lifecycle management.
